Outdoor & hunting

The hunting set.

Two tools carefully chosen for the active hunter. A strong, flexible No 16 for pulling game or securing cargo, and the hunting leash that is the only leash you will ever need.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

The No 16 is the rope you reach for when the animal is down. A running loop around the legs, a fixed loop for the hand, and you drag without a knot to slip. Join two ropes together or make a body loop when the ground is heavy.

The hunting leash has been developed over years of testing in the field: light, adjustable, controlled semi-choke, no noisy chains or hooks. The handle opens into a large loop that fits around the upper body, so both hands are free for the rifle.

In the pack

  • 3.5 m RunLock No 16, fluorescent orange
  • RunLock hunting leash, 1.8–2.7 m
  • Instruction leaflet with the four locks

Why RunLock

  • Drag game without a knot that slips or jams
  • Body loop for pulling with your weight
  • Hands-free leash with controlled semi-choke
  • Fluorescent orange, seen in the undergrowth
