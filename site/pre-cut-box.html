The pre-cut box.

For shops and wholesalers. A counter box with 24 ready-cut RunLock ropes, easy to set up and the perfect try-out kit for customers who have not held a loop rope before.

No 8 is enough for most situations and a great all-round tool: keep a door open, hang the tools on the belt while standing on a ladder, tie the bike to the rack. The box stands on the counter and sells itself.

The box is also available with 24 washing lines of 7 m, or 24 deer drags of 2 m. Contact us for prices and delivery.
