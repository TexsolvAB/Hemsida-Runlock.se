The hunting leash.

A light, adjustable leash with controlled semi-choke. The neck loop adjusts in seconds to fit any dog, and the handle opens into a large loop that fits around the upper body for hands-free work.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

The leash is one piece of RunLock: no metal, no chains, no hooks that rattle in the forest. The neck loop is a running loop with a stop, so it tightens only as far as you set it. Move the stop one loop to change the size.

Put the handle loop over your shoulder or around your body and the dog walks with you while both hands are free. It weighs next to nothing, fits in a pocket, and goes in the washing machine after the season.

In the pack

  • RunLock hunting leash, 1.8–2.7 m, adjustable
  • Instruction leaflet

Why RunLock

  • Controlled semi-choke, adjusted one loop at a time
  • Hands-free: the handle becomes a body loop
  • Silent: no chains, no hooks
  • Made in one piece, washable at 40 °C
  • Light enough to forget in the pocket
