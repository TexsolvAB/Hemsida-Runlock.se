Uses

One rope. Most jobs.

Six places the rope earns its keep, with the size and the lock for each.

Towing & recoveryA running loop over the hitch, a quick loop through the recovery ring, and a rope that stretches a quarter of its length before the pull comes on.
Cargo securingOver the load, around the rail, through a loop and pull.
HuntingA rope that drags game without slipping, joins to a second rope when the ground is heavy, turns into a body loop for pulling, and leads the dog with a leash that makes no noise.
DogsA light, soft leash that adjusts to the dog in seconds, never tangles, and has nothing that rattles, rusts or breaks.
Boat & dockA mooring line that adjusts one loop at a time, a fender line that moves in seconds, and a rope that does not swell, stiffen or rot in salt water.
Camp, home & gardenA washing line that holds the hangers, a tarp line that pulls tight, a tool sling, a tether for the mower, a rope to keep the gate open.

Much more than just a rope. Made by Texsolv AB in Tösse, Sweden, since 1983.
