How it works

The running loop.

The everyday RunLock lock. The rope runs through one of its own loops, so the noose tightens under load and opens again when you slack it off. Over a tow hitch, a post, a bundle of firewood or a piece of game.

Watch the steps →Shop RunLock
Step by step

4 steps. No knot.

The animation plays one step at a time and pauses. Click a step to jump to it, drag the slider to go back and forth, or play the whole sequence.

Pictures

Every step, frame by frame.

1

Lift a fold of the rope where you want the loop.

2

Open a loop where you want the running loop to sit.

3

Push the fold through one of the rope’s own loops.

4

Done. Pull the long end to tighten the loop, slack it off to open it.
