Outdoor & hunting

The outdoor set.

Three ropes for an active outdoor life. No 16 for dragging and lifting the heavy stuff, No 8 for tying down and strapping equipment, and No 4 for hanging gear and clothes in the camp, or as a tent line.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

Three sizes, one system. The heavy No 16 takes a beating all day: pull by hand, make a body loop for better balance, or join two ropes for more pulling force. The No 8 is the all-round rope for strapping the load on the sled, the roof rack or the trailer. The thin No 4 is the camp line: hang the washing, the lantern and the food bag, or use it as a guy line for the tarp.

Everything is made without knots. A running loop, a securing lock, and the built-in stretch does the rest. When a rope gets dirty, wash it in the machine at 40 °C.

In the pack

  • 3.5 m RunLock No 16, fluorescent orange
  • 5 m RunLock No 8, fluorescent yellow
  • 7 m RunLock No 4, black
  • Instruction leaflet with the four locks
