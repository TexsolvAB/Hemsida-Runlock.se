How it works

The fixed loop.

A loop that keeps its size whatever the load. Use it for a carabiner, a ring, a hand grip that must not choke, or the neck loop of a leash.

Watch the steps →Shop RunLock
Step by step

5 steps. No knot.

The animation plays one step at a time and pauses. Click a step to jump to it, drag the slider to go back and forth, or play the whole sequence.

Pictures

Every step, frame by frame.

1

Push the end through one of the rope’s loops, so you get a loop of the size you want.

2

Close up: the end sits in one loop, and the next loop on the end lies right beside it.

3

Push the end through that next loop as well.

4

Pull the loop tight.

5

Locked. The loop keeps its size under load. Push the end back out to release.
