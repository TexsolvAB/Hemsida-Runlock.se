The dog leash.

For dog lovers and outdoor people. A light, adjustable leash with controlled semi-choke, a neck loop that adjusts in seconds to fit any size, and nothing that rattles or tangles.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

The leash is one piece of RunLock. The neck loop is a running loop with a stop: it tightens only as far as you set it, and you move the stop one loop at a time to fit a puppy or a full-grown dog. The handle is a fixed loop that stays the size you made it.

Because there are no swivels, clips or chains, there is nothing to break, nothing to rust and nothing to make noise. When it is muddy, put it in the washing machine at 40 °C.
