Sizes and colours

One rope, six sizes.

The number is the size, the millimetres are the diameter, and loop length, strength, stretch and weight all grow with it. No 4 hangs the washing; No 20 tows the car. Five colours, the same rope in all of them.

Buy 10 m in any size →How the figures were measured
The six sizes

Size, strength, stretch.

Breaking strength is the published rating, set with a margin below the force measured at SP. Stretch is the elongation at break in the same test.

Which size

Pick the size by the job.

When in doubt, take the next size up: a thicker rope is kinder to the hand, kinder to the load and has strength to spare.

No 4 · 4 mm

The thin one. Washing lines, tent and tarp lines, hanging gear and clothes in the camp, crayfish traps. 7 m weighs less than 100 g.

Washing line → · Outdoor set →

No 6 · 5 mm

Leashes, fender lines, light tie-downs, a tether at the camp, long lines by the metre. The size of the leashes.

No 8 · 6 mm

The all-round size. Cargo on the trailer, the bike on the rack, the canoe on the roof, the gate, the ladder. The size in the pre-cut box and in every vehicle set.

No 12 · 8 mm

Heavier tie-downs, longer runs, larger boats and swell, dragging by hand. Comfortable to pull on.

No 16 · 10 mm

The hunting rope. Dragging game, lifting, pulling by hand with a body loop, quads and garden tractors.

No 20 · 12 mm

The towing line. Cars, 4x4, snowmobiles, heavy dragging and recovery, with 25 % stretch to soften the pull.
