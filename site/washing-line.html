The washing line.

The organiser for laundry, tools and equipment. Hang it horizontally, vertically or at 45 degrees. Fast to set up, easy to adjust, extend or shorten, and the loops hold the hangers in place. No pegs, no metal clips, no hooks.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

The loops hold the hangers firmly at any angle and keep the laundry apart, so it dries faster. Attach it to anything with the standard RunLock locks: a hook, a tree, a balcony rail, a tent pole. The built-in stretch makes it easy to pull tight.

It is just as useful for tools in the workshop, for gear in the camp, or for keeping the door open in the house. Soft, pliable and washable.
