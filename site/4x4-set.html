The 4x4 set.

Strength and flexibility for vehicles. No 20 is the heavy-duty rope for towing and heavy dragging, No 8 the all-round rope for securing cargo or hanging gear in the garage. An indispensable set for the off-road driver.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

The No 20 goes over the hitch with a running loop, or through a recovery ring with the quick loop, and its stretch takes the snatch out of the pull. The No 8 straps the jerry cans, the shovel and the hi-lift to the rack with a securing lock, and holds the bonnet or a door open in the field.

Both ropes are soft polyester: nothing that scratches the paint, nothing that rusts, and nothing that jams when it is wet or frozen.

In the pack

  • 5 m RunLock No 20, fluorescent orange
  • 5 m RunLock No 8, fluorescent yellow
  • Instruction leaflet with the four locks

Why RunLock

  • Towing and recovery with built-in stretch
  • Cargo secured without ratchets or hooks
  • Connect and disconnect in seconds, also with gloves
  • Will not jam under load, wet or frozen
  • Fluorescent colours, easy to find in the mud
