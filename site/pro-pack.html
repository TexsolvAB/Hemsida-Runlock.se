Home, work & pro

The pro pack.

The all-round rope for professionals and anyone who uses a lot of it. Ten metres of RunLock in the size and colour you need. Create grips, snares and locks in an instant, join two ropes to extend, attach carabiners and tools, and adjust the length in seconds.

Buy in the webshop →Which locks to use

Six sizes from the 4 mm No 4 to the 12 mm No 20, five colours, all in ten-metre lengths. The thin sizes come in blister packs, No 16 and No 20 hank-wound. Choose the size by what you pull and the colour by what you need to see.

The Pro series is what our professional users buy: forestry, farming, rescue services, boat yards and workshops. Full breaking strength in every loop, tested by RISE, and the built-in stretch that lets you tie down with force.

In the pack

  • 10 m RunLock in the chosen size and colour
  • No 4 to No 12 in blister pack, No 16 and No 20 hank-wound

Why RunLock

  • Six sizes, five colours, one system
  • Full strength in every loop
  • Join two ropes to extend, cut to shorten
  • Attach carabiners, hooks and tools without knots
  • Washable, UV- and salt-water resistant
