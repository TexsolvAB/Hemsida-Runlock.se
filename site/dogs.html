Uses

One leash for every dog.

A light, soft leash that adjusts to the dog in seconds, never tangles, and has nothing that rattles, rusts or breaks. Washable when it is muddy. The same rope also ties the dog to the tree at the camp and joins two leashes for two dogs.

Dogs

A leash without hooks, chains or tangles

The neck loop is a running loop with a stop. Slide the stop one loop and the collar is one loop bigger; it tightens under pull only as far as the stop allows. That is the controlled semi-choke: a gentle correction, never a strangling one. The handle at the other end is a fixed loop that keeps its size.

The dog leash is 1.8 m and made for the daily walk. The hunting leash is longer, 1.8 to 2.7 m, and its handle opens into a loop that fits around the upper body for hands-free work in the forest. Both are made in one piece, weigh next to nothing, and go in the washing machine at 40 °C.

At the camp or the trailhead, a running loop around a tree turns the leash into a tether. Two leashes joined loop to loop walk two dogs on one hand.

Which size

  • Dog leash1.8 m, daily walks
  • Hunting leash1.8–2.7 m, hands-free
  • No 6A tether or a long line by the metre
